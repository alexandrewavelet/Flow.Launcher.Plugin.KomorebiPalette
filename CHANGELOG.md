# Changelog

## 1.0.0

First release.

- Window actions: promote to main tile, toggle float / monocle / maximize, send to workspace, go to app.
- Workspace actions: nine layouts, restore the layout from komorebi.json, rename, zen mode, no gaps, reset gaps, toggle tiling, toggle window stacking.
- komorebi actions: pause, reload configuration, restart, force restart, open komorebi.json and whkdrc.
- "Go to" results show the app's official name (Windows Terminal, Sublime Text…), searchable by name or exe.
- Forgiving search: prefixes, abbreviations, typos and accents.
- Context menu: copy the komorebic command, open its documentation.
- Detects an unresponsive komorebi and offers a force restart.
- Settings for paths, editor, restart flags, zen gaps, app results and action delay.
